# macOS-style icons

These icons come from the **WhiteSur icon theme** by Vince Liuice and
contributors (https://github.com/vinceliuice/WhiteSur-icon-theme), licensed
under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (see `LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`).

The `.svg` files are the original vector sources from the theme, renamed for
this site and minified with SVGO. The `.webp` files are rendered from those
SVGs at 2× display size, which is what the site loads.
